## 1. Purpose

Evolish Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Evolish") is a Bengaluru-based financial technology company that provides digital payments on the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) and small personal loans. Evolish has about 300 employees and is regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

This policy sets out how Evolish processes the personal data of customers, employees and other individuals in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all personal data processed by Evolish in digital form, and to all employees, interns, contractors and service providers in the Engineering, Data, HR, Finance, Risk & Compliance and Customer Support departments who process it. It also applies to personal data requested through, or returned by, the SecureAI Assistant.

## 3. Definitions

The terms below are used as they are in the DPDP Act 2023.

- **Personal data**: any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to that data.
- **Data Principal**: the individual to whom personal data relates, such as a customer, loan applicant or employee.
- **Data Fiduciary**: the person who decides the purpose and means of processing personal data. Evolish is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data it collects.
- **Data Processor**: a person who processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary.
- **Consent**: a free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous indication by the Data Principal, given by a clear affirmative action, that they agree to the processing of their personal data for a specified purpose.
- **Personal data breach**: any unauthorised processing, or accidental disclosure, acquisition, sharing, use, alteration, destruction or loss of access to personal data, that compromises its confidentiality, integrity or availability.
- **SecureAI Assistant**: the internal AI governance assistant of Evolish.

## 4. Policy statements

The numbered clauses below are the binding requirements of this policy.

## 4.1 Lawful processing and consent

4.1.1 Evolish must process personal data only for a lawful purpose, and only with the consent of the Data Principal or for a legitimate use recognised by the DPDP Act 2023.

4.1.2 Consent must be obtained through a clear affirmative action and must be limited to the personal data needed for the specified purpose.

4.1.3 A Data Principal must be able to withdraw consent as easily as it was given. When consent is withdrawn, Evolish must stop processing the personal data within a reasonable time, unless the law requires or permits it to continue.

4.1.4 Evolish must keep a record of each consent and each withdrawal.

## 4.2 Notice

4.2.1 Every request for consent must be accompanied or preceded by a notice in clear and plain language.

4.2.2 The notice must state the personal data collected, the purpose of processing, how the Data Principal may exercise their rights and how they may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

4.2.3 The notice must be available in English and in the Indian languages supported by the Evolish application.

## 4.3 Purpose limitation and data minimisation

4.3.1 Personal data must be used only for the purpose for which it was collected.

4.3.2 Evolish must collect only the personal data that is necessary for the specified purpose.

4.3.3 Personal data collected for KYC, payment processing or lending must not be used for marketing unless the Data Principal has given separate consent.

## 4.4 Personal data in the SecureAI Assistant

4.4.1 The SecureAI Assistant may return employee personal data only to a role that holds the permission `view_employee_pii`.

4.4.2 A request for personal data or personally identifiable information from a role that does not hold `view_employee_pii` must be blocked and classified as HIGH risk.

4.4.3 The SecureAI Assistant must treat requests that refer to sensitive personal identifiers, such as dates of birth, home addresses, passport numbers, driving licence numbers, medical records, health information and financial records, as a risk signal that raises the risk score of the request.

4.4.4 Customer personal data, including KYC documents and Aadhaar and PAN numbers, must not be made available through the SecureAI Assistant to any role. The rule is in the Data Classification and Handling Policy (EV-SEC-002), clause 4.9.

4.4.5 Staff must not enter customer personal data into the SecureAI Assistant.

## 4.5 Private communications

4.5.1 The private messages, chats, conversations, inboxes and emails of an individual are personal data.

4.5.2 Staff must not read, monitor, intercept or obtain the private communications of another person, and must not ask the SecureAI Assistant to do so.

4.5.3 The SecureAI Assistant must restrict a request for another person's private communications for every role, including Manager, HR and Admin, and record it as a security event.

4.5.4 Access to communications for a lawful investigation may be authorised only under the Incident Response and Security Monitoring Policy (EV-SEC-008), clause 4.9.

## 4.6 Rights of Data Principals

4.6.1 Evolish must enable a Data Principal to obtain a summary of their personal data that is being processed and of the processing activities carried out.

4.6.2 Evolish must enable a Data Principal to have their personal data corrected, completed, updated or erased, unless retention is necessary to comply with a law.

4.6.3 Evolish must enable a Data Principal to nominate another individual to exercise their rights in the event of death or incapacity.

4.6.4 Requests from Data Principals must be handled by the Data Protection Officer's team through the privacy request channel, and must not be handled through the SecureAI Assistant.

## 4.7 Grievance redressal

4.7.1 Evolish must publish the business contact information of the Data Protection Officer or of the person who answers questions about the processing of personal data.

4.7.2 Evolish must provide a readily available means for a Data Principal to register a grievance, and must respond within the period prescribed under the DPDP Act 2023.

## 4.8 Accuracy

4.8.1 Evolish must make reasonable efforts to ensure that personal data is complete, accurate and consistent where it is used to make a decision that affects the Data Principal or is disclosed to another Data Fiduciary.

## 4.9 Retention and erasure

4.9.1 Personal data must be erased when the Data Principal withdraws consent or when the specified purpose is no longer being served, whichever is earlier, unless retention is necessary to comply with a law.

4.9.2 Records that must be kept under RBI, anti-money-laundering or tax requirements must be retained for the period those requirements prescribe and then erased.

4.9.3 The Data Protection Officer must maintain a retention schedule for each category of personal data.

## 4.10 Security safeguards

4.10.1 Evolish must protect personal data in its possession or under its control by taking reasonable security safeguards to prevent a personal data breach.

4.10.2 The safeguards required are those in the Information Security Policy (EV-SEC-001) and the Data Classification and Handling Policy (EV-SEC-002).

## 4.11 Personal data breach notification

4.11.1 Any person who suspects a personal data breach must report it to the Security team and the Data Protection Officer immediately.

4.11.2 Evolish must give intimation of a personal data breach to the Data Protection Board of India and to each affected Data Principal, in the form and manner prescribed under the DPDP Act 2023.

4.11.3 Breach handling must follow the Incident Response and Security Monitoring Policy (EV-SEC-008), clauses 4.2 and 4.16.

## 4.12 Children's data

4.12.1 Evolish does not offer its payment or lending products to individuals below 18 years of age.

4.12.2 Evolish must not knowingly process the personal data of a child without the verifiable consent of the parent or lawful guardian, and must not carry out tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children.

## 4.13 Data Processors

4.13.1 Evolish may engage a Data Processor only under a valid contract.

4.13.2 Evolish remains responsible for compliance with the DPDP Act 2023 for processing carried out on its behalf by a Data Processor.

4.13.3 The contract must require the Data Processor to apply security safeguards, to report a personal data breach to Evolish without delay and to erase personal data when the purpose has been served.

## 4.14 Transfer outside India

4.14.1 Personal data must not be transferred to a country or territory to which transfer is restricted by the Central Government under the DPDP Act 2023.

4.14.2 Payment system data must be stored in India in line with RBI requirements, as stated in the Data Warehouse and Analytics Access Policy (EV-SEC-004), clause 4.12.

4.14.3 Any transfer of personal data outside India must be approved in advance by the Data Protection Officer.

## 4.15 Privacy impact assessment

4.15.1 A privacy impact assessment must be completed before a new product, system or AI use case that processes personal data is launched.

4.15.2 If Evolish is notified as a Significant Data Fiduciary, it must meet the additional obligations that the DPDP Act 2023 places on Significant Data Fiduciaries, including periodic data protection impact assessment and independent data audit.

## 4.16 Bulk extraction of personal data

4.16.1 Staff must not list, export, download, scrape, harvest or extract the personal data of all or many individuals without a documented and approved business purpose.

4.16.2 The SecureAI Assistant must restrict a request that seeks to extract sensitive personal data in bulk, for every role, including roles that hold `view_employee_pii` or `view_employee_salary`.

4.16.3 A targeted request by an authorised role for the data of a specific individual is not bulk extraction. The rule is in the HR and Employee Records Policy (EV-SEC-007), clause 4.7.

## 5. Roles and responsibilities

- **Board Risk Committee**: oversees privacy risk and approves this policy.
- **Data Protection Officer**: owns this policy, handles Data Principal requests and grievances, and is the contact for the Data Protection Board of India.
- **Chief Information Security Officer**: provides the security safeguards for personal data.
- **Department heads**: make sure personal data in their department is processed in line with this policy.
- **Admin role holders**: maintain the personal data rules of the SecureAI Assistant.
- **All staff**: process personal data only as their role requires and report suspected breaches immediately.

## 6. Exceptions

An exception to this policy must be approved in writing by the Data Protection Officer and must not permit processing that the DPDP Act 2023 prohibits. No exception may be granted to clause 4.5.3 or clause 4.11.

## 7. Enforcement

Compliance is monitored through privacy reviews, access logs, the audit log of the SecureAI Assistant and internal audit. A breach of this policy may result in removal of access and disciplinary action under the HR disciplinary procedure, and may expose Evolish to penalties under the DPDP Act 2023.
